# Support Services evidence review — 2026-10-25

Reviewed all 20 exact titles assigned to this disjoint batch. Healthcare Administrator belongs to the informatics batch; Biomedical/Clinical Engineer and Biomedical/Clinical Technician are reviewed here once and retain their biotechnology appearances in the canonical map.

## Coverage

- 20 authored mapping reviews, 18 entries with at least one supported attribute, 2 with no supported assessment attribute.
- 34 supported attribute records: 29 regular, 5 setting-dependent. These are mostly soft interest cues, not evidence of universal requirements.
- No new hard-exclusion evidence. The one physical-assistance record is explicitly setting-dependent because Transport Technician is ambiguous.
- Dietary Aide and Environmental Services have no assigned attributes; all unlisted attributes remain unknown, not absent.

## Method

Opened BLS occupational profiles and read duty/subtype sections. Queried O*NET for actual reported-title matches, then read occupational task sections. Used SSH, HSPA, ANFP, AHE, NCI and VA role definitions where the chart label needed more specificity. No inferred clinical exposure from a pathway, title keyword, infection-control task, emergency repair urgency, or generic workplace risk. No aggregate O*NET context score was converted into a universal condition. Evidence notes are paraphrases; citation URLs are preserved per claim. Review date records retrieval/review, not source publication date.

Soft cues describe task content that could appeal to a preference. They are not validated personality scores, ability thresholds, or proof that every job performs the task. Runtime integration should preserve mapping scope and moderate confidence for broader groups. Appointment scheduling for navigators was not used for the group-project planning question because that would overextend the question's meaning. Workday pace, predictable routine, task switching and continuity remain unknown without directly relevant evidence.

## Mapping cautions

- Dietary Aide is reported under multiple O*NET groups: food preparation, dietetic technicians, and counter workers. No single occupation selected.
- Environmental Services is a department/personnel umbrella; AHE explicitly describes both. No single occupation selected.
- Transport Technician has no confirmed exact match. Orderlies is only a possible patient-transport family; lifting is conditional, no emergencies inferred.
- Telehealth Presenter is a function within clinical jobs in the VA source, not a universal occupation. Digital cue remains conditional.
- Biomedical/Clinical Technician is not silently equated to all biomedical technicians; the cited source specifically concerns biomedical equipment repair.
- Combined BLS profiles were read at the relevant subtype. Interpreter data do not import translator-only software tasks; facilities manager data do not import records-manager tasks.
- Surgeon-like blood exposure, medical emergencies and personal care were not inferred for any support role.

## QA

Exact assigned IDs/titles checked against support-inventory.json; 20 unique IDs, no missing or additional records. All claims have status, source, date, scope, confidence and evidence note. No runtime, article or Sheet edits. Parent is responsible for independent spot-check and integration.
