# Therapeutic evidence research — 2026-10-25

Reviewed all 54 assigned exact inventory titles; source labels and IDs remain unchanged. This is not 54 distinct occupations and not complete verification of every preference.

52 titles have at least one supported attribute; 2 combined labels have no assigned attribute. There are 118 supported attribute records: 91 core/regular and 27 setting-dependent. Missing attributes remain unknown, never absent. Some supported records are deliberately restricted to soft ordering and broader groups.

Method: read BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook What They Do / Duties and relevant occupational subsections; read O*NET qualitative task lists for acupuncturists, anesthesiologist assistants, dietetic technicians, and ophthalmic medical technicians. Read relevant professional scopes for orientation and mobility, pedorthics, wellness coaching, and patient care technicians; Mayo's healthcare career description for dialysis. No numeric O*NET scores, title keywords, pathway stereotypes, or student aptitude inferred. Generic workstyle pair questions (pace, team, variety, focus, location, continuity, help) remain untagged because polarity and frequency cannot safely be inferred from these sources.

BLS combined pages were used only when duties explicitly apply to the named role/subsection. Examples: CNA identified by BLS as a nursing-assistant title; PTA uses assistant duties, not aides; CRNA does not receive nurse-midwife duties. Clinical duties are not universalized to broad physician, nursing, psychologist, PA, or social-work roles. Kinesiotherapist/Kinesiologist remains ambiguous after checking AKTA and AKA; art/music/dance remains a combined label after checking separate O*NET art and music occupations. PCT evidence explicitly says 'some or all' tasks, so all PCT attributes are setting-dependent. OT teaching self-care is not equivalent to routine bathing assistance. Animal care is separate from human personal care and positioning.

Hard-exclusion candidates are limited to high-confidence, exact/alias role evidence. Core emergencies: EMT, Paramedic, Athletic Trainer. No new regular/core blood attribute. Regular wounds: Surgical Technologist (working during surgery and at incision sites). Physical assistance and personal care are independent fields; runtime owner must preserve optional controls and unknown eligibility. Even an occupation-specific usual duty does not claim every individual workplace.

Source access: web tool successfully read cited sources on review date. Direct Python HTTP retrieval of BLS failed with proxy HTTP 403; browser-backed web reading succeeded, so no research blocked. O*NET 29-1129.03 URL was inaccessible; no dance-therapy claim taken from it. No app, article, Sheet, runtime, student message, or deployment edits.

QA: JSON parsed; 54 unique IDs exactly match assigned inventory. Every supported record carries status, source URL, reviewedAt, confidence, source note, and scope. Independent spot-check verified all 17 regular/core comfort candidates. Reviewer requested stronger PTA lifting/moving evidence and explicit disclosure of the limited surgical-incision inference; both applied.
