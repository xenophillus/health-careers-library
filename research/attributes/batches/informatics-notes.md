Method: read BLS occupational duties, O*NET occupation descriptions/reported titles/tasks, and professional-association descriptions. Broader mappings remain setting-dependent, even where a task is typical of the candidate group. Such cues can be displayed as scoped research leads; an integrator should not assign the weight of an exact verified occupational duty or use them for hard exclusion. Clinical data specialist, data miner, account, finance, quality and service labels are not forcibly assigned to the nearest recognizable occupation. Their mapping notes identify why a job-specific scope is still needed. This is review of every inventory label, not full verification of every label's attributes.
