**C — Medical Information Technologist.** The review title is “Health Information Technologist (related occupation).” The article explicitly describes related work; exact synonymy with the NCHSE label remains unproven. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/health-information-technologists-and-medical-registrars.htm) and [O*NET29-9021](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/29-9021.00) support the related occupation. No accepted exact-title coverage credit or silent consolidation of other information careers.

**D — Radiologic Technician.** The general-radiographer mapping can proceed: [O*NET29-2034](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/29-2034.00) explicitly reports Radiologic Technician and Radiographer titles. The article excludes equivalence with limited-scope X-ray roles and automatic CT, mammography, PET or radiation-therapy qualifications. [ARRT radiography](https://www.arrt.org/pages/earn-arrt-credentials/credential-options/radiography), [ARRT education](https://www.arrt.org/pages/earn-arrt-credentials/initial-requirements/primary-requirements/education-requirements-primary), and [Kansas exemptions](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/laws/065_000_0000_chapter/065_073_0000_article/065_073_0004_section/065_073_0004_k/) establish those boundaries. The four original source appearances across B/C/D remain intact.
